# Page Topology

Source: `https://learningseo.io/`

The page is a single long-form route with one centered content column and three full-bleed bands.

1. `HeaderNavigation` — absolute above the hero, fixed after 100px scroll; search and two-level menu.
2. `HeroSection` — oversized title, roadmap version, introduction, animated dotted SEO illustration.
3. `RoadmapSection` — gradient heading cap and 12 independent expandable roadmap cards.
4. `ResourceBanner` — blue/cyan Google Sheets callout.
5. `VideoGallery` — 18 playable YouTube cards; two columns on desktop and one column at tablet/mobile.
6. `FaqSection` — 12 independent expandable answers.
7. `TipsSection` — lavender full-bleed band with 40 masonry cards and share popovers.
8. `FooterSection` — newsletter strip, sitemap, social links, legal and credits.

## Responsive topology

- Desktop reference: 1440×1000 viewport, 11,579px document height.
- Tablet reference: 768×900 viewport, 20,485px document height.
- Mobile reference: 390×844 viewport, 19,129px document height.
- Main content width: 85% with a 1224px maximum at 1440px.
- Mobile main gutter: 20px; roadmap and full-bleed media deliberately extend to the viewport edges.
- Video layout switches to one column at 1024px. Header, hero, roadmap, FAQ, tips and footer use their compact layouts below 768px.

## Shared design tokens

- Typeface: Mukta, weights 300, 400, 700 and 800.
- Navy: `#000036`; purple: `#a87be9`; blue: `#2e73ea`.
- Body text: `#606060`; dark text: `#303030`; page background: `#f2f2f2`.
- Newsletter: `#efe5ff`; cards: `#ffffff`.
- Desktop body: 17px; mobile body: 14px.
